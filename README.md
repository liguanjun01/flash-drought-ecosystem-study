Code for Response of ecosystem productivity and stability to flash droughts across moisture gradients in China

Data Availability：All datasets utilized in this study are publicly accessible and free to download from their respective official platforms. Specifically, root-zone soil moisture data were derived from the ECMWF ERA5-Land reanalysis product (https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/); solar-induced chlorophyll fluorescence (SIF) and gross primary productivity (GPP) were obtained from the GOSIF dataset (http://data.globalecology.unh.edu/); the Global Aridity Index (AI) was extracted from Version 2 of the Global Aridity Index and Potential Evapotranspiration (ET₀) Database (https://figshare.com/articles/dataset/Global_Aridity_Index_and_Potential_Evapotranspiration_ET0_Climate_Database_v2/7504448/5); and the 2020 land use and land cover dataset (CNLUCC, abbreviated as LULC) was provided by the Resource and Environment Science Data Center, Chinese Academy of Sciences (http://www.resdc.cn/). Given the large storage volume of the original raw data, readers may visit the corresponding official websites to obtain the complete raw files as required. The code used for this study is publicly available at https://github.com/liguanjun01/flash-drought-ecosystem-study.
